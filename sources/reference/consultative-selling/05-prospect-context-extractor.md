# 05 — Prospect Context Extractor Schema

The minimum viable prospect context needed to make a lead conversation-ready.

Not a full research dossier. The practical prospect intelligence layer that turns visible context into usable sales support.

## Why this matters

Without prospect context, the AI can only guess.

With the right prospect context, the AI can:
- Assess likely fit
- Identify relevant messaging angles
- Understand tone and communication style
- Spot identity cues
- Recognise comment opportunities
- Judge whether to DM, comment, hold, or re-engage

The goal is not to over-research. The goal is to collect the right signals.

## What this extractor should always capture

### 1. Prospect Basics

- Full name
- Role
- Company
- Location if relevant
- LinkedIn profile link if available

### 2. Headline and Positioning Summary

- LinkedIn headline
- How they position themselves
- What they appear to want to be known for

### 3. About Section Notes

- Key themes from the About section
- Repeated language
- Values or standards signalled
- Commercial focus areas

### 4. Company Context

- What the company appears to do
- Market/category
- Size or maturity if visible
- Any obvious growth or change signals

### 5. Recent Content Signals

Review 3–4 recent posts where possible:
- Main themes
- Recurring topics
- Tone of communication
- Signs of growth, frustration, hiring, change, or opportunity

### 6. Communication Style Signals

- Direct or reflective
- Practical or visionary
- Formal or conversational
- Analytical, relational, educational, or authority-led

### 7. Visible Identity Cues

- How they describe themselves
- What standards they appear to hold
- What kind of authority they are building
- Whether they signal growth, reinvention, caution, innovation, or stability

### 8. Likely Priorities and Pain Points

- Likely priorities
- Likely pressures
- Likely bottlenecks
- Likely goals

Important: Separate observed facts from working assumptions.

### 9. Buying Signals and Readiness Indicators

- Active growth language
- Hiring
- Expansion
- Problem awareness
- Openness to partnerships
- Engagement with relevant topics

### 10. Risks, Sensitivities, and Watchouts

- Likely resistant to generic outreach
- Highly polished authority brand
- Low posting frequency
- Anti-sales posture
- Signs of overwhelm

### 11. DM Status

Examples:
- No DM yet
- Connected, no conversation
- Early conversation
- Active conversation
- Dormant conversation
- No reply after outreach

### 12. Comment Opportunity Assessment

- Whether they are posting actively
- Whether there are strong comment opportunities
- Whether public engagement would warm the relationship
- Whether commenting is the best rescue move if DMs are dormant

### 13. Operator Observations

Human observations not obvious from the raw profile:
- Feels highly networked
- Likely values precision
- Seems open but time-poor
- May respond better to peer framing than consultant framing

## Minimum viable standard

At minimum, this extractor should answer:
- Who the prospect is
- How they position themselves
- What they are talking about currently
- What tone they use
- What they may care about
- What the current DM status is
- Whether commenting is a strong move

If these are unclear, the thread is not yet conversation-ready.
