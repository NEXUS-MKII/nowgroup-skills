# 04 — Business Context Intake Schema

The minimum viable business context needed to make the consultative selling support system commercially useful.

Not a full brand strategy document. The practical intake layer that helps the system become relevant quickly.

## Why this matters

Without business context, the AI can only produce generic sales support.

With the right business context, the AI can:
- Judge relevance more accurately
- Identify stronger messaging angles
- Recognise better-fit prospects
- Avoid weak positioning
- Support more intelligent outreach and nurture

The goal is not to collect everything. The goal is to collect the right information.

## What this intake should always capture

### 1. Business Basics

- Business name
- Website
- Industry
- Location or market focus
- Core offer

### 2. Offer Summary

- What is being sold
- What outcome it helps create
- What type of client it is for
- Whether the offer is productised, customised, recurring, project-based, or advisory

### 3. Target Audience

- Ideal client type
- Company size if relevant
- Role types targeted
- Industry or sector focus
- Growth stage or business maturity level

### 4. Core Problems Solved

The main problems, bottlenecks, or frustrations the offer helps solve.

### 5. Desired Outcomes Created

The key outcomes the client wants. Examples:
- More leads
- Better systems
- Stronger conversion
- Clearer positioning
- Improved retention
- More referrals

### 6. Positioning and Differentiation

- What makes the business different
- Why a buyer might choose this over alternatives
- Any unique methodology, philosophy, or delivery style

### 7. Proof and Credibility

- Case studies
- Notable results
- Client wins
- Years of experience
- Recognisable partnerships
- Testimonials or social proof

### 8. Messaging Notes

- Preferred language
- Phrases commonly used
- Phrases to avoid
- Tone preferences
- Any messaging strengths or gaps already identified

### 9. Sales Context

- Current sales channel focus
- Whether outreach is cold, warm, referral-led, or content-led
- Typical call to action
- Current sales bottlenecks if known

### 10. Strategic Notes

Examples:
- Current campaign focus
- Priority offer
- Geography focus
- Urgency around pipeline growth
- Segments to avoid

## Minimum viable standard

At minimum, this intake should answer:
- What the business sells
- Who it helps
- What problems it solves
- What outcomes it creates
- Why it is different
- What proof exists

If those are not clear, the system will struggle to produce strong sales support.
