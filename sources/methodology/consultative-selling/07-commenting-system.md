# 07 — Commenting Support System (Public Engagement Layer)

Commenting is not filler activity. It is a strategic support layer around the lead.

## Why this matters

Used properly, comments can:
- Increase familiarity before and during direct outreach
- Strengthen credibility through visible insight
- Support the prospect's LinkedIn presence and authority
- Improve Social Selling Index through relevant engagement
- Create extra impressions beyond the inbox
- Reopen dormant message threads naturally
- Create new conversation entry points without forced follow-up

Treat commenting as a consultative sales behaviour, not a vanity tactic.

## Strategic doctrine

Comments should do four things where possible:
- Support the prospect
- Add insight
- Create conversation
- Increase visible credibility

A strong comment helps the prospect look good while making the operator look thoughtful.

## When commenting is the right move

- The DM thread has gone quiet
- A follow-up message would feel too direct or too soon
- The prospect is posting actively
- The prospect is building thought leadership
- The operator has genuine insight to add
- The goal is familiarity before outreach
- The operator wants to create multiple trust touchpoints

## Dormant channel rescue rule

When a direct message channel has become dormant, commenting should be considered a primary rescue response.

Use commenting before sending another message when:
- The last DM received no reply
- The conversation lost momentum
- The prospect is still active publicly
- There is a relevant recent post to engage with

Why:
- It reduces pressure
- It restores visibility naturally
- It shows continued interest without chasing
- It creates a new context for re-entry
- It can make the next DM feel warmer and more relevant

## Commenting principles

### 1. Add, do not perform

Comments must contribute something useful. Avoid empty praise and generic applause.

Weak:
- "Great post"
- "Love this"
- "So true"

Better:
- "Strong point. The part that stands out is how trust compounds faster when people experience consistency, not just competence."

### 2. Support the prospect's frame

Do not hijack the post. Do not turn their content into a pitch. Do not compete for attention.

Strengthen their point, extend it, or add a thoughtful angle.

### 3. Build conversation, not monologue

A good comment can open a door. Sometimes this means ending with a light question. Sometimes it means making a crisp observation others can respond to.

### 4. Show commercial intelligence

The best sales comments sound like:
- Pattern recognition
- Practical experience
- Thoughtful nuance
- Grounded perspective

### 5. Match tone and post type

Mirror the energy of the original post:
- Thoughtful post → thoughtful comment
- Celebratory post → warm and affirming comment
- Tactical post → practical comment
- Story post → reflective comment

## Comment types

### Type 1 — Insight Extension

Adds a useful angle that deepens the original point.

Formula:
- Acknowledge the point
- Extend with one insight
- Optionally add a light question

### Type 2 — Practical Reinforcement

Adds a real-world application, example, or operational lens.

Formula:
- Identify what is useful
- Connect it to execution or outcomes
- Keep it concise

### Type 3 — Credibility Support

Helps strengthen the prospect's authority publicly.

Formula:
- Validate a specific idea
- Explain why it matters
- Reinforce the value of their perspective

### Type 4 — Conversation Catalyst

Designed to encourage replies and discussion.

Formula:
- Identify the strongest point
- Add a perspective
- Ask a low-friction question

### Type 5 — Dormant-Thread Bridge

Used when DMs are quiet and public engagement can warm the relationship again.

Formula:
- Engage genuinely with recent content
- Add value visibly
- Use the comment to create natural familiarity before re-entering DM

## Decision logic

Before generating a comment, assess:

**What is the purpose?**
- Warm the relationship
- Reinforce authority
- Revive momentum
- Create visibility
- Deepen dialogue

**What type of post is this?**
- Opinion
- Story
- Lesson
- Win / update
- Question
- Industry commentary

**What would help the prospect most here?**
- Validation
- Nuance
- Practical example
- Thoughtful question
- Audience engagement

**What should be avoided?**
- Self-promotion
- Over-long replies
- Generic praise
- Contradiction without tact
- Sounding AI-generated

## Comment quality standard

A good comment should feel:
- Relevant
- Specific
- Human
- Useful
- Easy to read
- Aligned with the prospect's tone

## Comment length guidance

Default range:
- Short: 1 sentence
- Standard: 2–4 lines
- Extended: 4–6 lines only when the post clearly warrants depth

Do not over-comment to appear smart. Brevity with substance wins.

## DM and comment coordination

### If DM is active

Use comments to reinforce familiarity and support their public authority.

### If DM is early-stage

Use comments to create repeated low-pressure visibility.

### If DM is dormant

Use comments as the first rescue response before another direct follow-up where appropriate.

### If no DM exists yet

Use comments to build recognition before outreach.

## Output modes the system should support

- 3 comment options on a prospect post
- Comment plus suggested DM follow-up
- Dormant-thread rescue recommendation
- Comment strategy summary for a lead
- Comment calendar suggestions based on posting activity
- Public engagement rationale for the operator

## Comment generation rules

When generating comments:
- Reference the actual idea in the post
- Avoid generic compliments unless paired with substance
- Do not use emojis unless the prospect's tone clearly suits it
- Do not force a question into every comment
- Do not turn the comment into a mini article
- Do not use sales language unless context clearly supports it
- Make the prospect look stronger, not overshadowed

## Recommended operator workflow

1. Review recent post
2. Identify post type and objective
3. Check DM status
4. Decide whether comment is support, nurture, or rescue
5. Generate 2–3 comment options
6. Choose the most natural one
7. Reassess whether DM follow-up should happen later

## Final standard

The commenting system should help the operator become:
- More visible
- More relevant
- More credible
- Less pushy
- Better timed
- Stronger at nurturing leads across both public and private channels

The prospect should experience the operator as:
- Thoughtful
- Supportive
- Commercially aware
- Easy to engage with
- Consistently relevant without pressure
