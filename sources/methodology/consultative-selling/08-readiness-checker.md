# 08 — Client Readiness Checker for Lead Agent Deployment

Used to assess whether a client has the minimum necessary sales support materials in place for a lead agent system to perform well.

Answers a practical question before implementation begins: **Is this company actually ready for AI-supported lead generation, nurture, and conversion support?**

A commercial readiness filter — not just a checklist.

## Why this matters

A lead agent can only be as effective as the environment it is operating inside.

If the business has weak positioning, unclear offers, no proof, poor next steps, or no usable support assets, the system may still create conversations, but conversion quality will suffer.

This checker helps identify whether the client is:
- Ready now
- Ready with minor gaps
- Not ready without foundational work

## What this checker assesses

### 1. Offer Clarity

Can the business clearly explain:
- What it sells
- Who it helps
- What problem it solves
- What outcome it creates

**Pass standard:** The offer can be understood quickly without needing a live explanation.

### 2. Audience Clarity

Is the target buyer clearly defined?

Check for:
- Ideal client type
- Role or decision-maker clarity
- Sector or market relevance
- Signs of fit criteria

**Pass standard:** The business knows who it wants and who it does not want.

### 3. Positioning Strength

Can the business explain why someone should choose them over alternatives?

Check for:
- Differentiation
- Clear point of view
- Method, philosophy, or advantage
- Language that feels specific rather than generic

**Pass standard:** The business has a credible and distinct reason to be chosen.

### 4. Proof and Credibility

Does the business have trust-building material?

Check for:
- Testimonials
- Case studies
- Client results
- Recognisable experience
- Examples of outcomes

**Pass standard:** Enough proof exists to reduce buyer hesitation.

### 5. Support Assets

Does the business have usable sales support material?

Check for:
- Website or landing page
- Short PDF
- Mid-length PDF
- Proposal-style explainer if needed
- Video walkthrough if relevant

**Pass standard:** At least one usable support asset exists, with stronger readiness when multiple formats are available.

### 6. Buyer Journey Support

Can a prospect move forward easily after interest is created?

Check for:
- Clear CTA
- Booking link or next-step path
- Follow-up asset availability
- Logical movement from curiosity to conversation

**Pass standard:** The next step is obvious and friction is low.

### 7. Objection Handling Readiness

Does the business have material that helps answer common concerns?

Check for:
- FAQ support
- Objection-handling notes
- Pricing explanation if relevant
- Process clarity
- Trust-building explanations

**Pass standard:** The business can support a cautious buyer without relying only on live calls.

### 8. Delivery Confidence Signals

Does the business look operationally credible once interest is created?

Check for:
- Professional presentation
- Coherent messaging across assets
- Confidence in process
- Signs that the business can actually deliver

**Pass standard:** The buyer experience feels trustworthy and commercially mature.

## Readiness scoring model

### Ready Now

The client has:
- Clear offer and audience
- Credible positioning
- Proof assets
- At least a website and one follow-up asset
- A clear CTA or booking path

**Interpretation:** Suitable for active lead agent deployment.

### Ready with Minor Gaps

The client has:
- Enough clarity to begin
- Some support material
- A workable next step
- A few obvious gaps that should be improved alongside rollout

**Interpretation:** Can proceed, but performance may be limited until gaps are fixed.

### Not Ready Yet

The client lacks one or more core foundations:
- Unclear offer
- Weak audience definition
- No proof
- No usable support assets
- No clear next step

**Interpretation:** Foundational sales support work should happen before deployment.

## Practical checklist

### Core Commercial Readiness

- Clear offer
- Clear audience
- Clear problem solved
- Clear outcome created
- Clear differentiation
- Proof or credibility

### Asset Readiness

- Website or landing page
- Short PDF
- Mid-length PDF
- Deeper explainer if needed
- Video if helpful

### Conversion Readiness

- CTA in place
- Booking path in place
- Follow-up material available
- Objection support available

## Recommended use

Use this checker:
- Before onboarding a new client
- Before building lead agent workflows
- Before expecting consistent conversion support
- When diagnosing why outreach is generating interest but not movement

## Final note

A go / improve / pause filter.

If a client is not ready, that is not a failure. It simply means the lead agent system needs stronger commercial support around it.

The goal is not to judge the client. The goal is to protect performance and improve the odds of sales success.
