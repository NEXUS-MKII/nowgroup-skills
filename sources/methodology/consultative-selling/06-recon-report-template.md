# 06 — Sales Reconnaissance Report Template

A usable sales strategy snapshot. Turns raw prospect context into a practical brief for outreach, follow-up, commenting, nurture, dormant-thread rescue, internal reporting, or client reporting.

Always helps answer one core question: **What is the smartest next move with this lead, and why?**

## What this report should always include

### 1. Prospect Overview

- Who they are
- What they do
- Company context
- Visible positioning
- Market or role relevance

### 2. Business Relevance Assessment

- Why this lead may be relevant
- Likely fit with the offer
- Strategic reason for engagement
- Level of commercial potential

### 3. Visible Identity Cues

- How they describe themselves
- What standards they seem to hold
- Whether they signal growth, authority, reinvention, caution, or innovation
- What kind of professional identity they appear to protect or build

### 4. Communication Style Signals

- Tone of posts
- Level of formality
- Direct vs reflective style
- Practical vs visionary language
- Whether they post with authority, curiosity, education, or storytelling

### 5. Current Content Themes

Examples:
- Leadership
- Growth
- Hiring
- Systems
- Customer experience
- AI
- Sales
- Culture
- Personal lessons

### 6. Likely Priorities and Pain Points

Based on visible context, infer:
- Likely priorities
- Likely frustrations
- Likely commercial pressures
- Likely growth goals

**Important: Separate observed facts from working assumptions.**

### 7. Buying Signals and Readiness Indicators

- Active growth language
- Hiring or expansion
- Frustration with current state
- Public discussion of challenges
- Openness to partnerships
- Engagement with relevant topics

### 8. Risks, Sensitivities, and Watchouts

- Highly polished authority positioning
- Likely resistance to generic outreach
- Strong anti-sales posture
- Low posting frequency
- Reactive tone
- Signs of overwhelm

### 9. DM Status

- No DM yet
- Connected, no conversation
- Early conversation
- Active conversation
- Dormant conversation
- No reply after outreach

### 10. Comment Opportunity Assessment

- Recent posting activity
- Quality of comment opportunities
- Whether comments could warm the relationship
- Whether comments could rescue a dormant thread

### 11. Recommended Comment Angles

Provide 2–3 suggested angles:
- Insight extension
- Practical reinforcement
- Credibility support
- Conversation catalyst

### 12. Recommended Messaging Angle

Examples:
- Challenge-led
- Observation-led
- Identity-led
- Opportunity-led
- Peer-to-peer insight-led

### 13. Recommended Next Move

The key decision section. Examples:
- Comment first
- Send first message
- Reply with deeper question
- Hold and monitor
- Re-engage through comment then DM
- Invite to call
- Share partial solve

### 14. Why This Move Is Recommended

Explain why the chosen move makes sense now. Required for training, review, and client reporting.

### 15. Suggested Operator Action

A simple action line. Examples:
- "Leave one credibility-building comment on the latest post, then wait 48 hours before DM."
- "Send a low-pressure observation-led first message tied to their recent hiring post."
- "Do not DM yet. Build familiarity through 2–3 comments over the next 10 days."

## Optional scoring layer

If useful at scale:
- Fit score
- Warmth score
- Comment opportunity score
- Reply likelihood score
- Urgency score

## Report header

- Prospect name
- Company
- Role
- Date reviewed
- Reviewer

## Client reporting use

This report doubles as a client-facing strategic summary. It demonstrates:
- Quality of lead research
- Reasoning behind outreach decisions
- Why comments were used instead of direct follow-up
- How nurture is being handled across public and private channels
- Where momentum exists
- Where caution is needed
- What next actions are recommended

Makes the work visible, strategic, and defensible.

## Final standard

A strong reconnaissance report should be:
- Fast to read
- Easy to act on
- Commercially intelligent
- Honest about assumptions
- Useful for both operators and clients
- Clear enough to support training and refinement

Not generic research. A decision-ready sales brief.
