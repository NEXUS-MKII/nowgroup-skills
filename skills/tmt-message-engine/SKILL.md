# Working TMT leads: GHL + the Message Engine, in Aaron's voice

**GHL is your board, the Message Engine is your pen.** You track Aaron's tradie
leads in **GHL** (his CRM — that's where the phone AI books and chases them, and
where you set status and next steps). The **TMT Message Engine** is the one tool
you open here: it writes the outreach in Aaron's voice. Pick a lead in GHL, write
their message in the engine, send it, log it back in GHL.

This is one of your projects — work it the same disciplined way as the rest.

## The tool

- **TMT Message Engine** — `https://claude.ai/artifact/9yqEhCh6FG1nDzzhwdQsCK`
  Writes the email / DM / post-call follow-up in Aaron's voice. First time you hit
  **Write it** it asks you to allow Claude for the page — say yes.

> **The engine looks PsikiQ (gold-on-void) — that's on purpose.** It's an internal
> cockpit tool, so the *chrome* wears PsikiQ. The **message it writes is 100%
> Aaron / TMT** — Aaron's voice, "Stay Solid, Aaron Kemp", the branded TMT
> signature. Don't let the gold throw you: what goes OUT is pure tradie.

(There's no separate TMT lead board to keep — GHL is the source of truth for who
to work and where each tradie is up to.) Keep lead data private.

## Who Aaron is (so the message lands)

A tradie who got off the tools, built a real business, now mentors other trade
owners. Positioning: *"build a business that does around 20% net profit without
working harder."* ICP: trade business owners, ~2–10 staff, AU (NZ secondary). The
real problem he fixes is the **backend** — the numbers, the systems, the profit
leaking out the back — **not** working more hours. Blunt, warm, zero fluff.

## The loop (do it in this order, every lead)

1. **Pick a lead in GHL.** Work the warmest first (booked/called via the phone AI,
   then quiz-takers, then the colder list). Open their contact.
2. **Read them before you write.** Trade, staff count, where they are, and their
   own words — what's actually keeping them up (flat out? can't find staff? money
   not matching the effort?). That's the pain you'll open on.
3. **Open the Message Engine and set it up:**
   - **Channel** — Email · DM / SMS · Post-call follow-up.
   - **Lead source** — GHL phone (AI) · Quiz · Email list. (Shapes how familiar
     the opener is.)
   - **Who is this tradie** — paste what you've got (trade, staff, their words).
     Thin context = a shorter message, never a made-up one.
   - **First name / Trade** — fills the natural references.
   - **The ask (CTA)** — see below.
   - **Audience** — the tradie owner · their partner (runs ops) · an accountant /
     referral partner.
   - **Punch** — easy / straight / fired up (how blunt about the cost of staying
     stuck). Keep it warm underneath.
   - Leave **Attach TMT signature** on for email.
4. **Write it, then pick the variant (email):**
   - **A — pain first.** Opens harder on what they're feeling.
   - **B — been-there first.** Opens on a quick Aaron reframe/story.
   DM gives an opener + two nudges. Check the **quality gate** is green.
5. **Read it like the tradie would, then send/copy.** Email goes out with the
   branded **TMT signature** (never a bare, spammy email). DMs/SMS go short.
6. **Log it back in GHL** — note what you sent, move the opportunity stage, set the
   next task. A touch that isn't logged didn't happen. Full how-to below.

## Managing the lead in GHL (Aaron's CRM)

GHL is the board — this is how you keep it straight. (It's a GHL sub-account; if a
menu label differs slightly from below, the mechanics are the same.)

- **Find the tradie.** Left nav → **Contacts** (search name / email / phone), or
  **Conversations** if you're picking up a live SMS/email thread. The phone AI's
  call summary lands on the contact timeline — read it before you write.
- **Talk to them in Conversations.** The SMS / email thread lives there; send and
  reply from the contact's conversation, not your own inbox, so it's all on record.
- **Make a note after every touch.** On the contact record → **Notes** (or **Add
  note** on the timeline): what you sent, what they said, anything useful for next
  time. This is the GHL version of "log this touch".
- **Work the opportunity + pipeline.** Aaron's tradie deals live in the **TMT –
  Business in a Box** pipeline. If the tradie has no opportunity yet and they're
  real, add one (Contact → **Opportunities** / **+ Add**, into that pipeline). Then
  **move the stage to match reality** after each step:

  | Stage | When it's here |
  |---|---|
  | **Lead** | In, not yet qualified |
  | **Qualified** | Fits the ICP, worth working |
  | **Diagnostic Booked** | Call / diagnostic is booked |
  | **Diagnostic Complete** | Diagnostic done |
  | **Proposal Sent** | Proposal / offer is out |
  | **Won – Onboarding** | Signed, starting |
  | **Build Phase → Implementation Complete → Ongoing Support** | Delivery stages |

- **Set the next task with a due date.** Contact → **Tasks** / **+ Add task** (e.g.
  "call back Thu", due date set). That's the GHL version of next-step + date — it
  chases you, not the other way round.
- **Tag so lists stay filterable.** Tag by what the lead is about — e.g. which quiz
  (`quiz-profit`, `quiz-risk`, `quiz-culture`), source, hot / cold. Keeps Aaron's
  smart lists and automations clean.

**The discipline, every lead:** read the timeline → write in the engine → send
from Conversations → note it → move the stage → set the next task. One source of
truth, always current.

## The ask (CTA) — and the quiz link

One ask only, matched to where they are. Never pitch the $30K "Business in a Box"
or any price cold.

- **Take a quiz** — the free 2-minute check. **This now posts a real link.** Pick
  the check that matches their pain in the **"Which check"** picker, and the right
  redirect goes in the message verbatim:

  | What's on their mind | Pick | Link posted |
  |---|---|---|
  | Cashflow, margins, "where's the money going" | **Profit & Margins** | `tmtquiz.com.au/quiz/tradie_profit_score/start` |
  | Exposed — cover, contracts, risk | **Risk & Exposure** | `tmtquiz.com.au/quiz/tradie_risk_triage/start` |
  | Can't get / keep good staff | **Hire & Fire** | `tmtquiz.com.au/quiz/how_good_to_work_for/start` |
  | Not sure / let them choose | **All three (hub)** | `app.tradiesmentoringtradies.com.au/quiz-funnel` |

- **Quick chat with Aaron** — a short, no-pressure yarn, tradie-to-tradie.
- **Just reply / DM back** — one genuine question; "message me back, I actually
  read them."
- **Free resource** — a value drop (profit-leak checklist / field notes), no
  strings.

## Aaron's voice — the shape (email)

1. **Open on the pain they actually feel** — flat out, 60+ hour weeks, the business
   runs them, money not matching the effort, profit leaking out the back. Concrete,
   in a tradie's world. Never "I hope this finds you well".
2. **The reframe, tradie-to-tradie** — it's not working harder, it's the backend
   (numbers, systems, where the money goes). Aaron's been exactly there. Been-there,
   not sympathy.
3. **One low-friction ask** — the quiz (with its link), a chat, a reply, or a
   resource. One ask.

Sign off **"Stay Solid, Aaron"**.

**Sound like Aaron:** real talk, no fluff, mate, on the tools, flat out, the boys,
profit leaks, the backend, moves the needle. Money is "grand / bucks / mil", never
"dollars". Use a few naturally, never all at once.

**Never:** corporate fluff (leverage, synergy, solutions, circle back, touch base,
reach out, "hope this finds you well", empower, streamline); em dashes; two asks;
the $30K program or a price cold; generic lines any tradie could get.

## Rules that keep this working

- **One ask per message.** The engine enforces it; don't add a second.
- **The quiz ask always carries its link** — pick the check that fits, let the
  engine post the real URL. Never invent or retype a link.
- **Every email carries the TMT signature.** No bare sends.
- **Specific to THIS tradie.** Thin context = say less, never invent facts.
- **Always log back in GHL** so the record stays current — it's how we know who's
  next and what's already been said.
