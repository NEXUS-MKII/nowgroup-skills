# New Zealand — WHS Framework Reference

A structured checklist of *what to verify and where*, anchored to HSWA 2015. **This is not a frozen snapshot of the law.** NZ WHS law is currently mid-reform via the Health and Safety at Work Amendment Bill — always run the Phase 1 live verification before asserting any duty, and assess against HSWA 2015 *as enacted* while flagging proposed changes as not-yet-in-force.

## The governing Act

**Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 (HSWA)**, administered by the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE); regulator is **WorkSafe New Zealand**. HSWA was deliberately modelled on the Australian model WHS Act, so the spine is shared — the same PCBU concept, primary duty, consultation/participation duties, and risk-management approach. Key supporting regulations include the Health and Safety at Work (General Risk and Workplace Management) Regulations 2016 and the Hazardous Substances Regulations 2017.

## The shared spine (≈70% — mirrors the AU model)

- **PCBU primary duty of care** — ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, the health and safety of workers and others. A self-employed PCBU must also ensure their own health and safety so far as is reasonably practicable.
- **Officer due diligence** — officers must exercise due diligence to ensure the PCBU meets its duties.
- **Worker duties** — take reasonable care; cooperate with notified policies; comply with reasonable instructions.
- **Worker engagement & participation** — duty to engage with workers so far as is reasonably practicable and to have practices giving workers reasonable opportunities to participate effectively in improving H&S on an ongoing basis. (NZ emphasises this "worker participation" framing strongly.)
- **Risk management** — identify and manage risks via the hierarchy of controls.
- **Notifiable events** — notify WorkSafe of notifiable deaths, injuries/illnesses, and incidents; **preserve the site** until an inspector authorises resumption; **keep a record of each notifiable event for at least 5 years**.

## The moving surface (VERIFY current status in Phase 1)

The **Health and Safety at Work Amendment Bill** was introduced **9 February 2026**. It is a **Bill, not yet law.** Proposed changes to be aware of and flag (but NOT assess against until enacted):
- A shift in the Act's purpose to prioritise **"critical risks"** — hazards likely to result in death, notifiable injury/illness/incident, or a listed occupational disease (proposed new s.22A and Schedule 1A).
- A new **"small PCBU"** category (fewer than 20 workers) directing their compliance focus to critical risks.
- Strengthening Approved Codes of Practice (ACOPs) as "safe harbours."

**Action:** in Phase 1, web-search whether the Amendment Bill has been enacted and what is currently in force. If still a Bill, assess against HSWA 2015 as enacted and add an "Important Notice" that reform is pending. If the client is a small PCBU, note the proposed critical-risk focus as relevant forward context.

## Standards

NZ uses **AS/NZS** joint standards heavily, plus NZS where standalone. Common references: AS/NZS 4453.3 (chainsaw protective clothing), AS/NZS 1891 (fall arrest), AS/NZS 1715/1716 (respiratory protection), AS/NZS 3000 (electrical). Verify the current edition where material.

## Licensing / competency (trade-dependent — VERIFY)

- **Certificates of competence** — e.g. for certain high-risk plant and hazardous-substances handling.
- **Electrical** — Electrical Workers Registration Board registration + practising licence; AS/NZS 3000.
- **Plumbing/gas/drainlaying** — Plumbers, Gasfitters and Drainlayers Board registration/licensing.
- **Asbestos** — Class A/B removal licensing under WorkSafe.
- **Adventure activities, forestry, etc.** — sector-specific regimes where relevant.

## NZ-specific operational notes

- **"beforeUdig"** (formerly "Dial Before You Dig" in AU) is the NZ utility-location service — reference it for ground-penetrating work, not the AU brand name.
- Worker participation/engagement documentation is weighted heavily in NZ — an audit should specifically look for evidence of *how* workers are engaged, not just a policy that says they are.

## NZ requirement checklist (generic-trades baseline)

Assess each as evidenced / partial / absent. Builder identifiers in brackets map to `whs-document-builder`.

1. Documented H&S policy, endorsed by an officer (`STD:WHS_Policy`)
2. Risk management / hazard ID procedure with hierarchy of controls (`STD:Risk_Assessment_Form`)
3. Safe work procedures / SWMS-equivalent for high-risk work (`STD:SWMS_Template`)
4. **Worker engagement & participation practices** (documented + evidenced as real)
5. Notifiable-event procedure incl. site preservation + 5-year record-keeping
6. Emergency response plan (`STD:Emergency_Response_Plan`)
7. First aid needs assessment & provisions (`STD:First_Aid_Needs_Assessment`)
8. Worker induction process (`STD:WHS_Worker_Induction_Checklist`)
9. PPE register & issue process (`STD:PPE_Register`)
10. Training & competency register incl. registrations/licences (`STD:Training_Competency_Register`)
11. Plant/equipment SOPs for each machine actually operated (`SOP:<Equipment>`)
12. Subcontractor / overlapping-duty-holder management (`STD:Subcontractor_Management_Policy`)
13. Hazardous substances register & SDS access (Hazardous Substances Regs 2017)
14. Specific-hazard policies as applicable: manual handling, working at heights, fatigue, confined space, hot work (`STD:*_Policy`, `PERMIT:*`)
15. Toolbox-talk / participation records (`STD:Toolbox_Talk_Record`)
