# Australia — WHS Framework Reference

A structured checklist of *what to verify and where*, anchored to the model WHS framework. **This is not a frozen snapshot of the law.** AU WHS law is harmonised-but-not-uniform and currently mid-reform — always run the Phase 1 live verification for the client's specific state/territory before asserting any duty.

## The shared spine (≈70% — applies in all harmonised jurisdictions)

These derive from the model WHS Act and are stable enough to assess against, though always cite the client's adopting Act:

- **PCBU primary duty of care** — ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable (SFAIRP), the health and safety of workers and others affected by the work.
- **Officer due diligence** — officers must exercise due diligence to ensure the PCBU complies (this is what a documented, endorsed system evidences).
- **Worker duties** — take reasonable care, comply with reasonable instructions and policies.
- **Consultation** — duty to consult workers, and to consult/cooperate/coordinate with other duty-holders.
- **Risk management** — identify hazards, assess risk, control via the **hierarchy of controls** (elimination → substitution → isolation → engineering → administrative → PPE), review controls.
- **Incident notification** — notify the regulator of notifiable incidents; preserve the site.
- **Records & training** — maintain training/competency records, provide information/instruction/supervision/training.

## The moving surface (VERIFY current status per state/territory)

The model WHS Act amendments published **5 December 2025** expanded incident notification to include serious psychological harm, violent incidents (assault/credible threats), notifiable suicides/attempted suicides, **work-related absences of 15+ consecutive days**, and additional dangerous-incident categories involving mobile plant and falls. **These do NOT apply until adopted by each jurisdiction, and adoption is uneven** — early in 2026 only the ACT had adopted. Other recent items to check: NSW audiometric/hazardous-noise testing requirements (Clause 58) commenced 1 Jan 2026.

**Action:** in Phase 1, web-search the client's state/territory + "model WHS amendments adopted" + current year. If you cannot confirm adoption, mark related findings `verification: unconfirmed` and `remediation_type: verify`.

Western Australia and Victoria are notable for historically differing from the model framework — treat them with extra verification care (WA adopted a modified model WHS scheme; VIC retains its own OHS Act 2004).

## Standards

AU uses **AS** and **AS/NZS** standards. Common ones the audit references: AS/NZS 4453.3 (chainsaw protective clothing), AS/NZS 1891 (fall arrest), AS/NZS 1801 (helmets), AS 2550 (cranes), AS/NZS 1715/1716 (respiratory protection), AS/NZS 3000 (electrical "wiring rules"). Cite the standard but verify the current edition if it's material to a finding.

## Licensing / competency (trade-dependent — VERIFY)

- **High-risk work licences (HRWL)** — e.g. forklift, EWP, scaffolding, rigging, dogging, crane operation. State-issued, nationally recognised.
- **Electrical licence** — state-issued; AS/NZS 3000 competency.
- **Plumbing/gas licence** — state-issued.
- **Chemical** — e.g. ChemCert/AQF3 for herbicide use (landscaping).
- **Asbestos** — removal licensing (Class A/B) where relevant.

## AU requirement checklist (generic-trades baseline)

Assess each as evidenced / partial / absent. This is the TMT baseline; add trade-specific items from the SKILL.md industry guidance.

1. Documented WHS policy, endorsed by an officer (`STD:WHS_Policy`)
2. Risk management / hazard ID procedure (`STD:Risk_Assessment_Form`)
3. SWMS for high-risk construction work, where applicable (`STD:SWMS_Template`)
4. Consultation & worker participation arrangements
5. Incident notification & investigation procedure (verify current notifiable categories for the state)
6. Emergency response plan (`STD:Emergency_Response_Plan`)
7. First aid needs assessment & provisions (`STD:First_Aid_Needs_Assessment`)
8. Worker induction process (`STD:WHS_Worker_Induction_Checklist`)
9. PPE register & issue process (`STD:PPE_Register`)
10. Training & competency register, incl. licences (`STD:Training_Competency_Register`)
11. Plant/equipment SOPs for each machine actually operated (`SOP:<Equipment>`)
12. Subcontractor management (if they engage subbies) (`STD:Subcontractor_Management_Policy`)
13. Hazardous chemical register & SDS access
14. Specific-hazard policies as applicable: manual handling, working at heights, fatigue, confined space, hot work (`STD:*_Policy`, `PERMIT:*`)
15. Toolbox-talk / consultation records (`STD:Toolbox_Talk_Record`)
